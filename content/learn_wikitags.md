## WikiTags

WikiTags are Wiki Links with a special Prefix which triggers a function. Linny
has a few WikiTags builtin, but it's also possible to register new custom
WikiTags.

### LIN, The Linny WikiTag

The LIN wikitags redirects to Linny taxonomies or terms. Here are two examples.

- [[LIN subject]] - open the `subject` taxonomy.
- [[LIN tags:note]] - open the term `note` in the `tags` taxonomy.

### DIR, The Directory WikiTag

- [[DIR /tmp/test-vim-dir]] - Opens or create a directory. 

### VIM, The Vim WikiTags

- [[VIM :help 42]] - Executes a vim command.

### SHELL, The shell WikiTag

- [[SHELL touch /tmp/test-vim-dir/hallo]] - runs a shell command, here creating a file.
